# Executive Summary

- **Need:** Group customers by category-spending patterns and describe the resulting segments.
- **Approach:** Tableau clustering across six product categories, followed by size, spend, income, and household profiling.
- **Result:** Two clusters across **2,240** customers; C1 has **711** customers and C2 **1,529**.
- **Value:** Supports segment-specific offer tests; no lift or causal effect is measured.
